Step 1 — photograph the film

How to get a clean shot

  1. Backlight the film Tape it to a lightbox, or hold it flat against a bright white screen or a window.
  2. Kill the glare Turn off overhead lights and keep your own reflection out of the film. No flash.
  3. Square on, filling the frame Hold the phone parallel to the film and move in until only the film is in shot — no desk, no hands.
  4. Tap to focus, then hold still Tap the middle of the chest, wait for it to sharpen, then shoot.
  5. Check before sending Rib edges should look crisp. If it is soft or washed out, retake it — the analysis is only as good as this photo.
